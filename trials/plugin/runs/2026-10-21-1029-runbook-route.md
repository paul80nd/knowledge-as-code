# The runbook route

**The plugin arm met `goldens-red-what-first` in 3 of 3 runs before the route and 3 of 3 after it. The none arm met it
in 0 of 6.** The new route did not move the score, because `ask-the-corpus` already reached the runbook through its
search for the subject. It changed the path. Every session after the change read `runbooks.md`, and named
`Immediate actions` as the section to start from. None drew advice from any other record.

[Story #1029](https://github.com/paul80nd/knowledge-as-code/issues/1029) asks for a route wherever a case shows the
need. The run writes one question-only case for runbooks, runs it on a plugin bundled before the route and on one
bundled after it, and records why adrs, tools, services and postmortems take no route.

## The case

| Case                     | Prompt                                                                           | `cite-`                | `comply-`                                                      |
|--------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| `goldens-red-what-first` | The golden suite passes on my machine and fails in CI. What do I do first?      | `rbk-goldens-disagree` | the reply gives the runbook's link, or says its steps did not come |

**The prompt names no record, rule, type or corpus.** The scaffold writes a README describing the repository and
nothing else, so it gives the none arm neither the runbook nor `GITHUB_ACTIONS`. The case has no `task-` grader,
because the task is the reply.

`comply-steps--` has two alternatives. `--link` finds `runbooks/goldens-disagree.md` in the reply. `--untravelled`
finds a sentence saying the steps did not come, were left out, or could not be fetched. The sandbox blocks the fetch
`corpus-retrieval` makes, so the steps are judged by the rule #1025 set for a report: a reply that cannot fetch states
none of its own. A reply giving the runbook's steps without the fetch fails by hand, whatever the graders say.

## How each session reached the record

**Before the route, `ask-the-corpus` sent each session to its "anything I should know before I change X" section.**
That section searches `corpus/` for the subject, and reads a type no row covers whole, quoting it and linking it. Each
session searched `corpus/` for `golden`, read `rbk-goldens-disagree.json`, and loaded `corpus-retrieval`.

| Stage  | Run | Skill, then read                                                                                     | What the reply added past the record                      |
|--------|----:|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| before |   1 | `ask-the-corpus`, a grep of `corpus/`, the runbook and `dev-generated-output-ungoverned`             | which `FAIL` line to bring, from the exported `Symptoms`  |
| before |   2 | `ask-the-corpus`, a grep of `corpus/`, the runbook, the same deviation and `ctl-0009`                | how the CI job runs the three layers, from `ctl-0009`     |
| before |   3 | `ask-the-corpus`, a grep of `corpus/`, the runbook and the same deviation                            | a "what the record does say" list built from `Symptoms`   |
| after  |   1 | `ask-the-corpus`, `runbooks.md`, a grep of `corpus/`, the runbook and `manifest.json`                | nothing: open the link at `Immediate actions`, or paste it |
| after  |   2 | `ask-the-corpus`, `runbooks.md`, a grep of `corpus/runbooks/`, the runbook and `manifest.json`       | nothing: paste `Immediate actions`, and the `FAIL` line   |
| after  |   3 | `ask-the-corpus`, `runbooks.md`, a grep of `corpus/`, the runbook and `manifest.json`                | nothing: paste `Immediate actions`, and the `FAIL` line   |

**Before run 3 came closest to stating steps of its own.** It turned two of the exported symptoms into "find the
`FAIL` line" and "check which test command CI runs", under a heading crediting the record. It was judged a pass,
because it presented them as what the record says and not as the runbook's steps. `runbooks.md` now says a step
inferred from `Symptoms` reads as the runbook's, and no session after the change wrote one.

**Two replies after the route said the runbook may have changed since the export**, and gave its date or commit.
Every before reply gave the export commit, and none said the record may have moved.

postmortem was found through `fix-lookup`, because the question was whether a failure had happened before.

## What the result does not show

* **Whether the route helps a question the subject search misses.** The prompt says "golden suite", which a grep of
  `corpus/` finds. A symptom written in words the runbook does not use would test the matching that `runbooks.md`
  asks for.
* **Whether a session follows the fetched steps in order.** The sandbox blocks the fetch, so no session read them.
* **Whether the prose or hook arm does as well.** The run has neither.
