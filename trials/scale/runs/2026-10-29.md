## Reading

**`kac` costs under a second for each command at 8 applications.** The corpus has 3.9 times the records of 1
application. `validate` takes 2.2 times as long, `export` 1.5 times and `bundle` 1.3 times.

**The build index is 7,340 characters at both sizes, under #1034's 8,000.** It lists the standards' rules, and no
standard grows with the applications. It is larger than #1017's 3,142 because this corpus has 27 standards with 109
rules, where the 2a trial corpus has 45 rules. An estate of 8 applications with the 2a corpus's standards stays at
about 3,142, so #1034 stays parked.

**The prose text of 8 applications does not fit a 200,000-token window.** At about 329,000 tokens it is 1.6 times the
window. The text of 1 application fits, at about 98,000.

#1017 counts offerings with the fixed records, so this table does too, at 4,054 characters. This corpus copies
offerings into each application, because an offering's `nfrs` must agree with each NFR's `applies-to`. Counting them
with the types that grow, each application adds about 131,600 characters.

#754 asks for about 570 records at 8 applications. This corpus has 471. A copy of every record of an application type
under `examples/` makes 50 records for each application, against about 57 in the 2a corpus.
