U13. No. Below 1.0.0, `^0.16.0` only admits the same minor version, so 0.17.0 is out of range, and `restore` never asks the registry while the lock still satisfies the range. Edit `version` to `^0.17.0` and commit the `resolved` line that the next restore rewrites.

U14. A folder source holds only the last version packed, so `restore` refuses a `resolved:` lock that names the earlier version. Move the lock along with the range to the new `content-version`, or delete `resolved:` and let the next restore write it.
Page: cli/restore.md
