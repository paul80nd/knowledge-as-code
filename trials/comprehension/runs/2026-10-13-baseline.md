## What the baseline shows

* **Accuracy is at its ceiling.** Every page states its answer, and each reader found nearly all of them. So a later
  run cannot show a trim improving accuracy. Accuracy shows only whether a trim cost an answer.
* **Words read is the measure that moves.** A reader searches with the question's own terms. A section that states a
  rule on fewer pages returns fewer hits, and each hit has less text around it.
* **Readers of one set can differ by a large margin.** Schema reference reader A read 7,771 words, and readers B and
  C read 4,792 and 4,933. Reader A read five pages in blocks of about 100 lines. Readers B and C searched each page
  first. The median sets reader A aside, where a mean of all three would sit at 5,832.
* **One draft question was dropped before the run.** `framework/shaping-a-type.md#deviations` says six sections make a
  deviation's argument, and counts `Scope` among them. `.schema/deviations.yaml` makes `Scope` optional.
