## What the run shows

* **Words read follows the pages a search hits.** Use it fell from 23,339 words to 18,559, and its median from 5,103
  to 3,761. Schema reference fell from 12,537 to 7,769, and its median from 4,933 to 4,105.
* **The framework's median moves within reader variance.** The section fell from 13,829 words to 12,068, and four
  medians over it ran from 4,067 to 5,003, either side of the baseline's 4,722. Every reader's first broad search drew
  38 to 91 lines from `shaping-a-type.md`. That page is still 5,768 words, and #845 rewrote it as explanation the
  framework may hold. A trim large enough to move the median would have to cut its why.
* **A search misses a rule stated in other words.** F10 and F05 were each lost by a reader who searched the right
  page for the wrong words, or stopped a paragraph short. Each fix put the rule where the reader looked, in the
  reader's terms.
